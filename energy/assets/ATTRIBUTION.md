## Chinese idiom explanations

957 explanations and available pinyin fields in `data/idioms.json` are drawn from [pwxcoo/chinese-xinhua](https://github.com/pwxcoo/chinese-xinhua), `data/idiom.json`, retrieved 2026-10-20. The source repository uses the MIT license; its copyright notice and license are retained in `CHINESE-XINHUA-LICENSE.txt`. The repository states that its data was collected from online sources.

36 unmatched entries have concise editorial explanations. Four original question misspellings were corrected with stable IDs and per-question `correction_note` / `content_version`: 山青水秀 → 山清水秀, 色弱死灰 → 色若死灰, 震憾人心 → 震撼人心, 合浦环珠 → 合浦还珠. Source examples were not automatically imported because many use archaic language or unsuitable contexts for children. Original answer letters are preserved; the correct option's character is updated where necessary.
